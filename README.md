# learn tcm with phoebe

Reading the Yellow Emperor: 黄帝内经 for curious moderns - six 45-minute sessions, by Phoebe Fu.

**Live site:** https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-tcm-with-phoebe/

## What this is

A guided first read of China's foundational medical classic, the Huangdi Neijing. The ideas
(yin-yang, five phases, the zang-fu body, living with the seasons), the famous passages in
原文 + pinyin + plain English, and an honesty rail that never blurs what the text says with
what modern evidence shows.

## The honesty rail

Every substantive claim carries one of three labels:

- **The text says** - a statement in the Neijing, with chapter attribution
- **Tradition holds** - later systematization or received practice, not in the Neijing
- **Research says** - modern evidence, study named, numbers exact

This is a reading course about a foundational classic and a living tradition. It is **not
medical advice**. In the words of the US National Center for Complementary and Integrative
Health: "Don't use TCM to replace or delay seeking conventional care."

## The sessions

1. **The book** - one emperor, one physician, two millennia
2. **Yin-yang 阴阳** - the operating system
3. **Five phases 五行** - the correspondence engine, with the live wheel
4. **Zang-fu 脏腑** - the body according to the Neijing (functional, not anatomical)
5. **Living with the seasons 四气调神** - the four regimens and 治未病
6. **The Neijing today** - what holds, what doesn't

## Format

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Each session: a 45-min live arc, inline SVG diagrams,
a 3-question quiz, and a pin-able cheat sheet. Session 3 hosts `tcm-live.js`, an interactive
five-phases wheel: click a phase for its correspondences, toggle the 相生 generating and
相克 controlling cycles. It visualizes the model and asserts no physiology.

Running project: the learner's own **Neijing map** (a one-page reference filled in session by
session) plus a daily one-line seasonal journal.
