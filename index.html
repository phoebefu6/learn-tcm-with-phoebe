🧩 The running project: your Neijing map. A one-page personal reference you fill in session by session - the yin-yang pairs, the five-phase correspondence rows, the zang-fu functions - plus a seasonal self-observation journal started in Session 1, in the spirit of 四气调神 (framed honestly as reflection, not therapy).
The honesty rail (strictest edition). This course teaches what a 2,000-year-old text SAYS - clearly labeled "the text says" or "tradition holds" - and separately reports what modern research shows, with numbers, where it exists. It is a reading course about a foundational classic and a living tradition. It is not medical advice, and nothing here replaces actual medical care.
